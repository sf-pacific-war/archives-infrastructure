Archival Infrastructure

A Future-Proof Archival Infrastructure

The archive functions as durable institutional infrastructure rather than a shared drive and spreadsheet stack. Historical materials require preservation-grade rigor to support long-term trust, strong discovery, and rights-aware governance. The target state protects source files and source meaning, supports trustworthy access, and remains sustainable and interoperable as collections, partner sources, metadata models, and AI-enhanced access continue to grow.

档案基础设施

一个面向长期的档案基础设施

这套架构把档案系统定义为一个面向长期的机构基础设施，而不是“共享网盘 + 电子表格”的组合。历史档案材料如果要实现长期可信、优质检索与合规治理，就必须满足保存级别的技术严谨性。目标状态是建设一个能够保护源文件与源语义、支持可信访问，并在馆藏规模持续增长、合作档案机构与高校持续贡献记录、元数据模型持续演进、以及 AI 支持翻译、转录、检索与问答时，仍保持可持续性与互操作性的档案基础设施。

What We Need to Protect

The system must preserve collection hierarchy, source-native description, digital representation, preservation files, rights and access state, and AI-derived access layers as distinct but connected parts of one governed record.

必须被保护的结构边界

系统必须把馆藏层级、来源原生描述、数字表征、保存文件、权限与访问状态，以及 AI 衍生访问层明确区分，同时在一个可治理的记录体系中彼此连接。

System Priority

Build the archival infrastructure before scaling discovery and AI. Let search, OCR, transcripts, translations, vectors, and summaries expand access, but never overwrite the canonical record or preservation evidence.

核心判断

先把档案基础设施建对，再去放大检索与 AI。搜索、OCR、转录、翻译、向量索引与 AI 摘要都可以扩展访问能力，但绝不能覆盖权威主记录（canonical record）或保存证据。

Direction
方向

Core Direction of the Program

项目的核心方向

The program centers on the system boundary, the authoritative record model, and the relationship between preservation, discovery, and partner interoperability.

这一架构总览的核心，是明确系统边界、权威记录模型，以及保存、检索与合作伙伴互操作之间的关系。

Core Direction

  • Adopt a canonical archival system of record for governed metadata and files.
  • Treat shared drives and spreadsheets as intake and coordination surfaces, not the long-term database.
  • Begin with one representative collection and one partner-held record stream.

核心方向

  • 建立一个承载治理性元数据与保存文件的档案权威主系统（canonical archival system of record）。
  • 把共享网盘和电子表格保留为采集入口与协作界面，而不是长期数据库。
  • 从一个代表性馆藏和一条合作伙伴记录线开始落地。

Implementation Character

Durable architecture boundaries remain stable while the implementation path stays replaceable.

实施特征

长期稳定的是架构边界，可替换的是具体实施路径，而不是固定的软件清单。

Why Change
为什么现在要改

Why the Current Pattern Will Not Hold at Scale

为什么当前模式在规模扩大后不可持续

Shared storage and spreadsheets are useful operational tools. They are not sufficient archival infrastructure once we need preservation evidence, rights governance, cross-institution exchange, and explainable discovery.

共享存储和电子表格作为日常操作工具有价值，但一旦系统需要保存证据、权限治理、跨机构交换与可解释检索，它们就不足以构成真正的档案基础设施。

Preservation risk
保存风险

Authenticity Becomes Hard to Prove

真实性会越来越难以证明

When preservation files, access copies, and staff edits live in the same loose file pattern, fixity, version history, and source provenance become difficult to audit.

当保存文件、访问副本和人工编辑都混在松散的文件结构中时，校验值、版本历史与来源链条都很难被稳定审计。

Governance risk
治理风险

Rights and Access Stay Fragile

权限与开放状态会持续脆弱

Publication status, restrictions, and review state should not depend on ad hoc columns or inferred search results. They need governed fields, decisions, and logs.

发布状态、限制条件与审核状态不应依赖临时列或推断出的搜索结果，而应依赖被治理的字段、明确决策与可追溯日志。

Discovery and AI risk
检索与 AI 风险

Search Quality Stalls Without Structure

没有结构，检索质量和 AI 都会很快碰顶

Reliable retrieval depends on clear entity boundaries, citations, and source relationships. If derived AI output is mixed back into the record, trust erodes.

可信检索依赖清晰的实体边界、引用位置与来源关系。一旦把 AI 衍生内容重新混入原始记录，系统可信度就会下降。

Shared drives and spreadsheets still have a role in temporary intake, coordination, and ingestion triggers. They do not remain the long-term system of record.
共享网盘和电子表格仍然承担临时采集、协作整理和导入触发器（ingestion trigger）等角色，但它们不充当长期的权威主系统（system of record）。
Target State
目标状态

Five Connected Layers of the Target Infrastructure

目标基础设施的五个连接层

The architecture separates responsibilities cleanly. Preservation protects evidence, discovery improves access, and AI operates as a replaceable enrichment layer rather than hidden authority.

这个架构需要把职责明确拆开：保存层保护证据，发现层提升访问能力，AI 作为可替换的增强层存在，而不是隐藏的权威来源。

  • 来源描述、人物机构、地点、事件、主题与权限字段

  • 格式信息与可审计的长期保管链条

  • 精确字段检索、词汇检索、语义检索与重排
  • 名称规范、过滤器、facet 与带证据的结果展示

  • OCR、ASR、翻译、实体抽取、向量嵌入与摘要

  • AI 扩展访问能力，但不能改写档案权威性
Operating Model
运行模型

Non-Negotiable Design Rules

不可退让的设计规则

These rules keep the system durable as collections multiply, partner records arrive in different schemas, and retrieval methods change.

即使馆藏持续扩张、合作方记录来自不同 schema、检索方法不断变化，这些规则也能确保系统保持长期稳定。

Separate Authoritative from Derived

把权威记录与衍生物分开

Source descriptions, rights decisions, and preservation files must not be silently overwritten by search or AI layers.

来源描述、权限决策与保存文件不能被搜索层或 AI 层悄悄覆盖。

Model the Historical Object Correctly

正确建模历史对象

Collections, objects, source records, representations, files, and segments need distinct identities and lifecycles.

馆藏、对象、来源记录、数字表征、文件与片段都需要独立身份与生命周期。

Preserve Differences; Harmonize for Discovery

保留差异，再为检索做协调

Keep source-native meaning where it matters, then map into a smaller canonical model for shared retrieval and analytics.

在关键处保留来源原生语义，再映射到较小的 canonical model 以支持共享检索和分析。

Federate Without Erasing Source Identity

联邦互通，但不抹平来源身份

Cross-institution discovery should preserve provider terms, rights, provenance, and the ability to cite the original source.

跨机构发现必须保留提供方术语、权限、来源链和对原始来源的可引用能力。

Design for Replacement

按“可替换”原则设计

Search engines, vector indexes, and AI models will change. Stable records and stable interfaces should let us upgrade without rewriting the archive.

搜索引擎、向量索引和 AI 模型都会变化。稳定的记录层与接口层应让系统能够升级而不必重写档案本体。

Canonical Model Boundary

The core record should distinguish context, thing, description, digital form, and stored file. That separation is what makes provenance, replacement, and precise citation possible.

Canonical Model 的边界

核心记录必须区分上下文、对象、描述、数字形态与存储文件。只有这样，来源链、可替换性与精确引用才真正成立。

Why This Boundary Matters

  • Prevents loss of source-native meaning when multiple institutions describe the same object
  • Allows OCR, transcripts, thumbnails, vectors, and AI output to stay replaceable
  • Supports precise citation down to page, region, clip, or timestamp
  • Creates a stable place to revise taxonomy and search logic without corrupting source evidence

为什么这个边界很重要

  • 避免多个机构描述同一对象时丢失来源原生语义
  • 让 OCR、转录、缩略图、向量索引与 AI 输出保持可替换
  • 支持精确到页面、区域、片段或时间码的引用
  • 在不破坏源证据的前提下，持续调整 taxonomy 和检索逻辑
Discovery and AI
检索与 AI

Hybrid Retrieval, Governed by Structured Facts

由结构化事实约束的混合检索

Deterministic metadata governs access, dates, rights, and hierarchy. Probabilistic retrieval improves discovery over text, language, and concepts. Both are necessary, but they must play different roles.

确定性的结构化元数据负责访问、日期、权限与层级；概率性的检索方法负责在文本、语言与概念层面提升发现能力。两者都需要，但职责必须不同。

1. Exact and Fielded Search

1. 精确与字段检索

Best for identifiers, names, dates, rights states, collection filters, and known-item retrieval.

最适合处理标识符、人名、日期、权限状态、馆藏过滤以及 known-item 查询。

2. Lexical Search

2. 词汇检索

BM25-style retrieval remains strong for rare names, historical phrasing, acronyms, and quoted language.

BM25 一类的词汇检索仍然非常适合罕见姓名、历史表述、缩写与精确短语。

3. Semantic Retrieval

3. 语义检索

Vector retrieval can connect multilingual wording, indirect references, and conceptually related materials across collections.

向量检索可以连接跨语言表达、间接提及和跨馆藏的概念关联材料。

4. Reranking and Evidence Packages

4. 重排与证据包

Final ranking should combine relevance, authority signals, and explicit evidence such as source record, page, region, or timestamp.

最终排序应结合相关性、权威信号与明确证据，例如来源记录、页面、区域或时间码。

AI assistants should answer over evidence packages, not over detached chunks without source context. Every AI-derived translation, transcript, extraction, or summary should carry citation, review state, and provenance.
AI 助手应基于带来源上下文的证据包回答，而不是基于脱离语境的孤立文本块。所有 AI 生成的翻译、转录、抽取与摘要都应带有引用、审核状态与来源信息。

Governance

治理

Every published record carries explicit provenance, rights, and access state.

所有已发布记录都带有明确的来源链（provenance）、权限（rights）与开放状态（access state）。

Preservation

保存

Preservation files have fixity, version history, and independent storage controls.

Preservation files 具备 fixity、版本历史与独立的存储控制。

Discovery

检索

Known-item and exploratory query sets are measured with metrics such as MRR and nDCG.

已知目标查询（known-item）与探索式查询集合使用 MRR、nDCG 等指标持续评估。

Interoperability and AI

互操作与 AI

Partner exchange works through governed APIs or exports, and all AI outputs remain labeled, reversible, and citable.

合作方交换通过受治理的 API 或导出完成，所有 AI 输出都保持可标注、可回退、可引用。

Standards and Implementation References

标准与实现参考

This direction aligns with current archival and digital preservation practice. The aim is not to adopt every standard in full, but to use the right ones for the right layer and keep mappings explicit.

这个方向与当前档案学和数字保存实践一致。目标不是把所有标准全部照搬，而是在合适的层使用合适的标准，并把映射关系保持明确。

Core Standards

  • OAIS: preservation repository concepts and functional boundaries
  • PREMIS: preservation events, agents, rights, and fixity evidence
  • DCMI: broadly interoperable descriptive terms
  • EAD: archival hierarchy and finding-aid structure
  • LIDO: richer object-oriented exchange for complex heritage materials
  • IIIF Presentation 3.0: interoperable image, AV, ranges, and annotations delivery
  • OAI-PMH: metadata harvesting for federation
  • OCFL and NDSA Levels: storage versioning and operational preservation practice
Official sources: OAIS · PREMIS · DCMI · EAD · LIDO · IIIF · OAI-PMH · OCFL · NDSA Levels

核心标准

  • OAIS：保存仓储的概念模型与功能边界
  • PREMIS：保存事件、参与者、权限与 fixity 证据
  • DCMI：广泛互操作的描述性术语
  • EAD：档案层级与 finding aid 结构
  • LIDO：适用于复杂馆藏对象的面向对象交换模型
  • IIIF Presentation 3.0：图像、音视频、范围与标注的互操作交付
  • OAI-PMH：联邦场景中的元数据 harvest 机制
  • OCFL 与 NDSA Levels：存储版本化与保存运营实践
官方来源： OAIS · PREMIS · DCMI · EAD · LIDO · IIIF · OAI-PMH · OCFL · NDSA Levels

Implementation Patterns Worth Studying

  • ArchivesSpace: archival description and hierarchy as a governed system of record
  • Archivematica: OAIS-aligned preservation workflow patterns
  • IIIF community practice: presentation and citation across institutions
