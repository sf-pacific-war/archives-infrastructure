Palette

Paper, navy, brass, and a restrained seal-red accent.

Paper and navy carry most of the composition. Brass guides attention. Red stays rare.

Paper `#F7F1E6` for main backgrounds and reading surfaces.
Memorial Navy `#21374D` for headers, anchors, and structural contrast.
Antique Brass `#B58A42` for keylines, highlights, and quiet emphasis.
Seal Red `#8B3C35` for rare editorial notes and exceptional states.
