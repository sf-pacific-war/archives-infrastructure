Project Structure

Archival infrastructure first, public access layered on top.

The project moves from program boundary to source truth, then to trusted public release and evidence-based AI access.

Each stage depends on the stability and clarity of the layer below it.

Program boundary

The four tracks define the scope of the archives infrastructure project.

Source of truth

Canonical records carry identity, source, rights, provenance, and review status.

Trusted public release

Approved record data supports search, filters, and clear record detail views.

Evidence-based AI

AI layers sit on top of evidence links, derived-content labels, and human review.

Core Principles

Keep the architecture durable while the tools evolve.

Canonical record first

Original records, source IDs, rights, review state, and publication status remain more stable than search or AI layers.

Derived layers stay labeled

Translations, summaries, entity extraction, and answers remain replaceable and clearly tied to sources and review status.

Federated by design

NARA, university archives, and local holdings can be discovered together through mappings and source identifiers.

Separate storage from access

Preservation masters, access derivatives, and public pages require different controls.
